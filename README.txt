Adds support for Maytronics Dolphin range pool cleaners with the MyDolphin Plus app.

To add your robot, sign in with the email address you use for the MyDolphin Plus app. Maytronics will email you a sign-in code to enter in Homey. Your robot must already be added to your account in the MyDolphin Plus app.

Maytronics asks you to sign in again with a new code about once a year. When that happens the device becomes unavailable; open the device settings in Homey and choose Repair to sign in again.
